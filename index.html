HOW TO PLAY

Controls

Rotate: ◀ ▶ or A/D. Thrust: ▲ or W. Fire: SPACE or the FIRE button. Your ship wraps around the screen edges.

Ships

Each ship has a different hitbox size and handling — bigger ships are easier to hit but often faster or tougher. Some unlock by reaching a level, others by completing a specific achievement — check the Achievements screen from the main menu.

Utility powerups (stack with anything, including each other)

🛡
ShieldStacks up to 3 charges. Each hit breaks one charge instead of costing a life. Charges don't expire — only hits use them up.
⇢
RangeStacks up to 5. Each stack sends your bullets further before they fizzle out.
⋔
SpreadStacks up to 5. Each stack adds a pellet fired in a fan. Combos with any weapon mode below — pairing it with Overcharge turns your shots into a short-range shotgun blast.
☇
Ray GunStacks up to 5. Passive auto-turret — automatically chains a single lightning bolt between nearby small asteroids within a fixed radius of your ship, no aiming needed. Each stack lets the chain bounce one hop further.

Weapon modes (only one active at a time)

These replace each other — grabbing a new one drops whatever you were running. Pick your playstyle.

⚡
OverchargeBullets fully destroy any asteroid in one hit, no splitting. Every shot kicks your ship backward.
»
Rapid FireTimed. Nearly doubles your fire rate.
➹
PiercingTimed. Bullets punch through up to 2 extra asteroids instead of stopping at the first.

Levels

Every level adds more, faster asteroids. Every 5th level is a hard checkpoint — extra asteroids and a speed spike, no warning needed.

Enemies

A UFO mothership drifts in periodically and fires wide, sloppy shots. Destroy it and two escape pods eject — smaller, faster, and much more accurate. UFOs drop powerups far more often than asteroids, so they're worth hunting down.

ACHIEVEMENTS

Each one unlocks a cosmetic recolor of a ship — same handling, different shine.
