HOW TO PLAY

Controls

Keyboard: Rotate with ◀ ▶ or A/D. Thrust: ▲ or W. Hold SPACE to fire.
Mouse (PC): Move your cursor — the ship aims toward it automatically. Hold right-click to thrust, hold left-click to fire.
Touch: Use the on-screen buttons. Edge wrapping is the default; enable Containment Walls in Settings if you want solid arena borders.

Ships

Each ship has a different hitbox size and handling — bigger ships are easier to hit but often faster or tougher. Some unlock by reaching a level, others by completing a specific achievement — check the Achievements screen from the main menu.

Weapon builds

Pick a weapon build before the run. Your ship and weapon are separate choices, so any hull can pair with any build.

Run upgrades

Upgrade windows appear twice per 10-level sector — after levels 3 and 7, then again after 13 and 17, 23 and 27, and so on. Choose 1 of 3 build-specific upgrades. Core upgrades can rank up repeatedly with diminishing gains, while every 10th-level boss awards a separate weapon EVOLUTION.

Universal pickups

Weapon-defining powerups are gone. Shields remain as a simple survival pickup so your build stays readable and focused.

Levels

Levels 5, 15, 25, etc. are special threat waves with different field compositions. Every 10th level is a boss fight. Clearing the Level 10 boss secures the run's salvage cash-out; dying before then recovers only 50%.

Test Ground

Sandbox mode for mechanic testing. Any ship can be used. Press 1–4 to swap instantly between Longbow, Scattergun, Rocket Pod, and Cycler with max upgrade ranks and boss evolutions enabled. Targets and shields restock automatically. Test Ground never awards achievements, unlocks, salvage, or permanent stat progress.

Enemies

UFO pressure rises with the run. Early motherships are simple threats; later fields introduce Gunner, Hunter, and Carrier variants that fire faster, pursue you, or deploy pods. Every 10th level replaces the normal field with a boss encounter.

ACHIEVEMENTS

Each one unlocks a cosmetic recolor of a ship — same handling, different shine.
