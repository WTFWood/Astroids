HOW TO PLAY

Controls

Keyboard: A/D rotate, W thrust, SPACE fires, and R manually reloads magazine weapons. Arrow keys still work.
Mouse (PC): Move your cursor — the ship aims toward it automatically. Hold right-click to thrust, hold left-click to fire. A secondary reload key or mouse side button can be assigned in Settings.
Touch: Push the left stick LEFT/RIGHT to rotate and UP to thrust; diagonals turn and thrust together. Hold FIRE on the right to shoot. Edge wrapping is the default; enable Containment Walls in Settings if you want solid arena borders.

Ships

BUILD CUSTOM HULL opens the Hull Workshop and is the default ship system. Nose, frame, wings and engine construct the ship around shared attachment points, while Reactor determines engine power and adds its own internal vector design. One hull-color slider controls the entire custom ship. The original Interceptor, Viper, Titan and Phantom unlock as achievement-gated recipes built from the same new parts, so they can be loaded and modified.

Weapon builds

Pick a weapon build before the run. Your ship and weapon are separate choices, so any hull can pair with any build.

Run upgrades

Upgrade windows appear twice per 10-level sector — after levels 3 and 7, then again after 13 and 17, 23 and 27, and so on. Choose 1 of 3 build-specific upgrades. Core upgrades can rank up repeatedly with diminishing gains, while every 10th-level boss awards a separate weapon EVOLUTION.

Universal pickups

Weapon-defining powerups are gone. Shields are deliberately scarce and only one loose shield can exist at a time. The first two shield layers each absorb two hits; a third layer is volatile, absorbs one hit, then detonates in a close-range overload burst that can vaporize nearby asteroids and enemy ships. Rare 1UP pickups add one life, up to a 5-life cap; they can drop from UFOs and are much more likely after boss kills.

Levels

Levels 5, 15, 25, etc. are special threat waves with different field compositions. Every 10th level is a boss fight. Clearing the Level 10 boss secures the run's salvage cash-out; dying before then recovers only 50%.

Test Ground

Sandbox mode for mechanic testing. Before entering, choose exact ranks for every weapon upgrade and Evolution — competing Evolution paths can be enabled together for stress testing. Inside the Test Lab, use the clickable spawner to create individual asteroid sizes, UFO variants, bosses, Ambushes, shields, and 1UPs on demand. Tap BUILD in the spawner at any time to change upgrade and Evolution ranks live, then APPLY & RETURN to resume the same test. Press 1–4 to swap weapon builds using each weapon's saved Test Lab preset. Test Ground never awards achievements, unlocks, salvage, or permanent stat progress.

Enemies

UFO pressure rises with the run. Early motherships are simple threats; later fields introduce Gunner, Hunter, and Carrier variants that fire faster, pursue you, or deploy pods. Every 10th level replaces the normal field with a boss encounter.
